SAFETY FIRST

A few habits that keep the celebration happy.

✓

Burst crackers only in open outdoor spaces, away from dry leaves and vehicles.

✓

Keep a bucket of water or sand within reach at all times.

✓

Light one cracker at a time, and never re-light a "dud".

✓

Children should use sparklers only under adult supervision.

✓

Wear cotton clothing — avoid loose, synthetic fabrics near flames.

✓

Store unused stock in a cool, dry place, away from open flame.
